# Economy lines

What the player reads and says when credits and things change hands, and
what a person says when nobody can answer for them in their own words. Each
`## key` lists the ways of saying one thing; the code picks one by the
person and the minute and fills in `{amount}`, `{name}`, `{item}`, `{what}`,
`{price}`, `{credits}`, `{gross}`, `{fee}`, `{net}` and `{weeks}`. Money is
credits, written `cr`; the minus sign is U+2212.

## notice-paid

- −{amount} cr paid to {name}

## notice-received

- +{amount} cr from {name}

## notice-given

- {item} handed to {name}

## notice-wage

- +{net} cr pay booked

## notice-wage-text

- Your week's pay of {gross} cr, less the {fee} cr file fee.

## notice-wage-weeks-text

- Your pay for {weeks} weeks of {gross} cr, less the {fee} cr file fees.

## notice-shift

- +{amount} cr for the shift

## notice-reward

- +{amount} cr from {name}

## notice-fare

- −{amount} cr fare

## notice-lifted-credits

- +{amount} cr lifted from {name}

## notice-lifted-thing

- {item} lifted from {name}

## notice-bribe-refused

- {name} noted your offer.

## notice-cannot-afford

- It costs {price} cr; you have {credits} cr.

- Hand over…

## label-credits

- Sell…

## prompt-lift

## say-thing

- Here, this is for you: {name}.

## say-credits

- Here, {amount} credits.

## say-pay

- Here are the {amount} credits.

## say-buy

- {name}, please.

## say-sell

- Would you give me {price} for {name}?

## accept-thing

- Thank you. I'll keep it.
- For me? All right.
- Nobody gives anything away any more. Thank you.

## refuse-thing-hostile

- Keep it. I want nothing from you.
- I don't take things from strangers.

## refuse-thing-wary

- What's that for? No, keep it.
- I'd rather not.

## refuse-thing-neutral

- That's not mine to take.
- No, thank you.

## accept-credits

- Thank you.
- I won't pretend I don't need it.
- That's bread for a day or two. Thank you.

## refuse-credits-hostile

- Put your money away.
- I don't want your money.

## refuse-credits-wary

- I don't need that.
- What is it for? No.
- Money from a stranger costs more later. No.

## refuse-bribe

- Put that away. I did not see it.
- I will note that you offered.
- People have gone missing for less. Put it away.

## accept-bribe

- I have not seen you.

## sold

- {price}. Here.

## bought

- I'll give you {amount} for it.

## refuse-buy

- I've no use for it.
- Not for me, thanks.

## refuse-serve

- I'm not serving you.
- Find somewhere else.
- Not you. Go.

## thanks-paid

- Entered.
- Good.
