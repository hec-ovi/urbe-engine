# Access lines

What a person says when the player asks them for access to a place they
hold a card for, or catches the player lifting one, and the labels and
notices the player reads. Each `## key` lists the ways of saying one thing;
the code picks one by the person and the minute and fills in `{place}`,
`{label}` and `{name}`.

## label-access

- Can you give me access to {place}?

## accept-card

- Here, take a copy of my card.
- All right. Don't lose it.
- Fine. Here's a card for {place}.

## refuse-card-hostile

- Access? To my place? Get lost.
- Not a chance.

## refuse-card-wary

- I don't hand out cards to strangers.
- I don't know you. No.

## refuse-card-neutral

- I'd rather not. Not yet.
- That's not mine to give away.

## refuse-card-unavailable

- Not now.

## notice-card

- Added to your inventory: {label}.

## notice-lifted

- You lifted the {label}.

## notice-caught

- {name} caught you taking the {label}.

## caught

- Hey! That's my card!
- Give that back, thief!
- Did you just... unbelievable.

## prompt-locked

- Locked: {place} needs an access card

## prompt-lift

- R  lift {name}'s key card
