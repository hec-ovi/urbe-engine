# CONTRACT: access

Purpose: every private space of the furnished buildings by a stable human address, the doors that lock them, who holds access to which, the access cards the player carries, and the people who caught the player stealing one.

## In

- `AddressBook({ buildings, nameOf })` ([Addresses.js](Addresses.js)): the live building sources (`parcelId -> { npc, interior }`) and `nameOf(parcelId) -> { name?, word?, street? }`, the building's own name, what it is (`apartment block`) and its street. Nothing is published for it: it derives every address at load from what the worlds already carry, Interior's numbered apartment entrances (`building.floors[].apartmentEntrances`) and each floor's layout rooms, so an existing world needs no reassembly.
- `PlayerAccess({ items, questCards?, book })` ([Access.js](Access.js)): the player's PlayerItems, the quest items they hold that open a door (`() => [{ id, name, scope }]`, [QuestDoors.js](QuestDoors.js) `questCards` over Quests `opens`) and the AddressBook.
- `PlayerItems` ([PlayerItems.js](PlayerItems.js)) `add({ id, kind, label, data, quantity? })`, `remove`, `restore(savedInventory)`; `Regard` ([Regard.js](Regard.js)) `drop(npcId, atMin)`, `restore(saved)`.
- `accessLines(markdown?)` ([lines.md](lines.md)), read as the companion's lines are.

## Out

- Addresses. A building is named by its own name, else `the <word> on <street>`, and on a card by its name or street (`Kessler Block`, `Third Street`). A floor is told as its apartment numbers count it: a number's leading digits are its storey (`1407` stands on floor 14), so `floor 14`, and with no numbered door the lowest floor above ground is the `ground floor`; below it `basement 2`. `building(parcelId)` is `{ parcelId, name, short, floors, units }`: dwellings first, one per numbered entrance (`apartment 1407`, its rooms the layout rooms of the entrance's unit, an upper duplex storey included), then the private rooms of each floor (rooms without a unit whose kind or role a card locks, below), numbered on their floor after the floor's apartments in plan order (`office 305`; on the ground floor `storage room G01`; below it `server room B2-01`). Each unit is `{ id, kind: 'apartment' | 'room', parcelId, floor, display, number, label, short, address, scope, rooms, doors }`, `address` the whole of it (`Kessler Block, floor 14, apartment 1407`) and each door `{ floor, position, inward, width, at, front }`, `front` the public side of the door where somebody stands at it. `unit(id)`, `home(npc)` (the simulation's `home.apartment`), `work(npc)` (the room of the post the person's role holds there, by its home anchor), `at(parcelId, [x, y, z])` (the floor, the dwelling or private room and the kind of room a point stands in, in words: `living room`, `reception`) and `find(text, parcelIds?)` (the units a line names by number).
- Scopes ([Access.js](Access.js)): `home:<parcel>/<home id>` one dwelling's entrance; `homes:<parcel>` every dwelling's in that building (a master card); `staff:<parcel>` its offices, executive offices, kitchens behind the counter and archives; `service:<parcel>` its stores and plant rooms; `security:<parcel>` its security, server and control rooms. Lobbies, galleries, corridors, receptions, shops, bars, cafés, dining rooms, open offices, meeting rooms, toilets, locker rooms, gyms and terraces have none. `scopesOf(npc, book)` is what a person holds: their own home's, and at their workplace by their post the scopes of the doors that building has: every worker `staff`; a cleaner `staff` and `service`; a receptionist `staff`, `service` and `homes`; security `staff`, `service`, `security` and `homes`; an executive `staff` and `security`. A building with no furnished floors has no locked door and gives no work card. `grants(held, scope)` opens a dwelling by its building's master card too. `scopeOfDoor(door)` is a door's own `scope`, else an apartment entrance's dwelling.
- Cards: `cardFor(scope, { book, issuer, how, atMin })` is the inventory item `{ id: 'card:<scope>', kind: 'access-card', label, data: { grants, access, issuer?, how, atMin? } }`, `label` what the card says on it (`Kessler Block 1407 key card`, `Third Street staff card`), `access` what it opens with the building, `how` `given`, `stolen`, `found` or `quest`. A copy of one scope is always the same item, so the player carries one card per scope. `describeScope(scope, book)` is the words: `label`, `place` (the door, for the prompt), `opens` (without the building, for the person who carries it) and `access`. `givesCard(disposition, scope)`: with no model, only a friendly person hands over a copy, and only of their own home's.
- `PlayerAccess`: `held()`, `holds(scope)` and `lockOf(door, feet)`: null for a door anyone opens, else `{ scope, place, locked }`, `locked` false while the player carries a card for it or stands on its private side (`door.inward` points in), so a lock never keeps anybody in.
- `PlayerItems.serialize()` is the save's inventory entries `{ id, name: label, quantity, state: { kind, data } }`; `restore` takes back the kinds it holds (`ITEM_KINDS`: `access-card`) and returns the rest, the quest items, untouched. The item model is general (`kind`, `label`, `data`) for the items to come.
- `Regard`: a person who caught the player lifting a card comes down one step of the disposition ladder each time (`adjust(disposition, npcId)`: friendly, neutral, wary, hostile); `serialize()` is the save's `access.regard` ([schema/access.schema.json](schema/access.schema.json), `urn:urbe:engine:access:access`). `notices({ disposition, heading, toPlayer, seed })`: a person facing the player always notices, otherwise as watchful as their disposition (hostile 0.7, wary 0.6, neutral 0.4, friendly 0.3), decided by the seed so the same moment goes the same way.
- Stories ([QuestDoors.js](QuestDoors.js)): `accessEvents({ entries, parcelId, scope, unitOf })` is the `accessed` event of each active Quests `access` step at a `door` that names the unit the player got into, and `questCards` the quest items whose `opens` names a door the building numbers, with its scope. `doorUnit(book, parcelId, door)` reads a door address by its label, else a number in it.

## The game

- Doors ([City](../city/CONTRACT.md)): an apartment entrance locks with its dwelling's scope; each private room's doorway, an open framed passage in Interior's layouts, stands a door the engine builds in that building's own pocket leaves, scaled to the doorway ([RoomDoors.js](../city/RoomDoors.js)), with the room's scope and label. Both lock, open, close by themselves after 5 s and collide as one kind of door; lift landings and cars are separate and never lock.
- The player ([Interactor](../player/Interactor.js)): aimed at a door locked to them, the prompt says what it needs (`Locked: apartment 1407 needs an access card`) with a shut lock mark, E leaves it shut and solid and a notice says the same; with its card the prompt reads `E  open the door to apartment 1407` with an open lock mark. Closing is never locked. R on an established person carrying a card the player lacks lifts it (`R  lift Mara's key card` on the prompt), their home's first.
- Walkers: an apartment's and a private room's doors are gates on a walk inside a building (InteriorRoutes), opened by the walker as it reaches them (Passage); the simulation sends people only to their own homes and posts, whose doors they hold, so no lock ever stops a walker. A leader who brought the player to a door by its address opens it on arrival when they hold its scope (companion `holds`).
- People: the chat's action row asks a person with identity for access to a door they hold a card for and the player does not, their home and then their work's (`Can you give me access to your apartment?`); a typed request carries a copy of every such card as talk `offers.give`. The person decides in their own words (Quests `give_item`), or with no model by `givesCard` after their regard. Given, the card joins the inventory with a notice under their name. A talk with a person with identity carries `addresses`: their home and work by address, where inside a furnished building their body stands now, the cards they carry and how often they caught the player stealing.

## Errors

- `PlayerItems.add` throws for an item without an id, a kind or a label.
- `E_COMPANION_LINES` from `accessLines` for a document that lacks a key or names an unknown field.
- An address the book cannot read leaves that building without addresses and its rooms without engine doors; nothing else fails for it.
