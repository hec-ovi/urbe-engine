# Contact and call lines

What a person says when the player asks for their number or calls them, and
the labels of the asks. Each `## key` lists the ways of saying one thing; the
code picks one by the person and the minute and fills in `{place}`.

## label-contact

- Can I have your number?

## label-meet

- Can you meet me here?

## accept-contact

- Sure. Here, put my number in.
- All right. Call me if you need anything.
- Here's my number. Don't make me regret it.

## refuse-contact-hostile

- My number? Get lost.
- Not a chance.

## refuse-contact-wary

- I don't hand my number to strangers.
- I don't know you. No.

## refuse-contact-neutral

- Maybe when I know you better.
- I'd rather not. Not yet.

## refuse-contact-unavailable

- Not now.

## call-greeting

- Hello?
- Yeah? I'm here.
- Hey. What is it?

## call-busy

- I'm at work. I can't talk now.
- Not now, I'm working. Call me later.

## notice-contact

- Added to your contacts. Call them from Contacts (P).

## name-here

- where I am
