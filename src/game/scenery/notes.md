# Scene notes

What anyone standing at a staged quest scene can see, handed to the dialogue model as the place's notes when a person has led the player there. Each `##` section is one key and holds one plain sentence. The notes open with the scene's `purpose-<purpose>`, then give one sentence for each body, prop and decal the scene still shows: its `pose-<pose>`, `asset-<mission asset family>` or `prop-<kind>`. A sentence says only what shows, never who someone is or what happened.

## purpose-crime-scene

It looks like a crime scene.

## purpose-aftermath

Something happened here not long ago.

## purpose-wake

People have come here to mourn someone.

## purpose-stakeout

Someone here is keeping an eye on things.

## purpose-ambient

Nothing out of the ordinary seems to be going on here.

## pose-death-a

A body lies on the ground.

## pose-death-b

A body lies on the ground, twisted where it fell.

## pose-wounded-crawl

## pose-ground-sit

## pose-kneel-examine

## pose-crouch-examine

Someone crouches there, studying the ground.

## pose-search-torch

Someone stands there searching with a torch.

## pose-grieving

Someone stands there crying.

## pose-standing-guard
