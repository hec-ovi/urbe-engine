# CONTRACT: player

Purpose: converts captured input into first-person movement and aimed interactions.

## Inputs and outputs

- `Input(element)` uses an attached HTML element. [Input schema](schema/input.d.ts) defines state, movement axis, mouse delta and capture result. `requestLock()` returns whether the request was accepted; `pointerlockchange` confirms `locked` and invokes `onLockChange(locked)`. Repeated pending requests share one promise. Rejected or detached requests settle false and permit a later explicit click.
- `axis()` returns normalized camera-relative movement; `drainLook()` returns and clears mouse movement; `consume(code)` returns one press; `endFrame()` clears presses; `clear()` clears held movement and zoom. `exitLock()` releases capture; `dispose()` removes listeners.
- `PlayerController({body,camera,input})` consumes [Physics player body](../physics/CONTRACT.md), a Three.js camera and Input. `update(delta)` advances stance, collision-constrained movement and camera pose. `lookAt(point)` sets heading; `turnTo(point, seconds = 0.45)` turns heading and pitch to the point the short way round, eased in and out over the seconds `update` advances; `beginRide(position,heading)`, `carry(position,heading)` and `endRide(position)` consume authoritative world coordinates from [agents](../agents/CONTRACT.md).
- `Interactor` consumes [city doors](../city/CONTRACT.md), the controller, [agents](../agents/CONTRACT.md), [simulation](../sim/CONTRACT.md), optional elevators, [quests](../quests/CONTRACT.md), [investigations](../investigation/CONTRACT.md) and [door collision](../physics/CONTRACT.md). `update(delta,questState)` returns the aimed prompt or null. `activate(clock,action)` applies the selected action; `close(clock,reason,{keep})` ends conversation. `talkTo(npcId,clock)` opens a conversation with the body that person has in the crowd, as E on them would, without aiming, turns the view to the person's face (`controller.turnTo`) and returns it; null while a conversation is open or when the person has no body or is fallen or retiring. `onConversation` carries the current conversation or null. `CHEST` is the height above a person's feet that aim is measured to. Talking names an anonymous body through its handle, or a live handle `Crowd.handleFor` finds when that one stopped answering, and offers the simulation the seed the body is drawn with, so the person keeps the look the player saw. A stress copy or a retiring body has no handle: it is talked to as nobody and gains no identity. The person being talked to faces the player for the whole conversation. Continuity refusing to take or release a body costs one press and falls back to the plain simulation interrupt and resume, never a thrown frame. On close, a body an open quest step still names (`quests.holdsCast`) or the host keeps (`keep`, somebody who agreed to come along) is held where it stands; everybody else walks back into their routine, after standing `LINGER_SECONDS` (5) where the talk left them turned to the player when the player left the talk (a fall that cuts it short lets them go at once).
- `PauseState` decides whether the player plays, has paused, or has the pointer free while the world plays on. It opens `paused`. `release(locked)`: the game lets the pointer go for UI of its own, remembered only while the player holds it. `lost(open)`: the browser reports the pointer lost, which pauses unless something of the game's own is open or it was released. `held()`: the pointer is taken, which plays and forgets the release. `update({ locked, escape })` once a frame: holding plays, Escape with the pointer free pauses. `holds(panel)` is whether the world holds still (paused, or a full panel open); `free({ locked, open })` whether the world plays on with the pointer free.
- When an exact cast identity carries an authored `characterName`, its prompt and conversation presentation use that name consistently with the objective. Dialogue receives a shallow presentation copy; the simulation NPC and anonymous bystanders retain their own names and ids.

## Invariants

Capture refusal leaves controls released and produces no unhandled promise rejection. Movement and camera follow the physical body. Door pivots and colliders follow the same published travel. Every door the Interactor runs, street, building and apartment doors alike, closes by itself `DOOR_CLOSE_AFTER` (5) seconds after it stood fully open or was last asked open (the player's E, or a walker marking it `asked`), and never while the player or a crowd member stands in its doorway (within half its width and 0.6 m of its middle, at its level). Optional `interiors` gates entrance opening on `floorShown` and requests a pending floor on E; its `apartmentDoors.doors`, the apartment doors of the floors shown now, run and are aimed at beside the city doors, gathered each frame into one array the Interactor keeps. Anywhere inside a lift car, Page Up and Page Down choose a floor without wrapping; E acts on the lift target under the crosshair, and a target publishing `aimRadius`, a car panel button or a landing's call button, is aimed at only while the crosshair ray passes within that radius of it. Aim selects the target in reach; near-equal aim favors the door.
